Your hand

Whole-number bets only. Your wager cannot exceed the chips in your bank.

Rules

Build a hand closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Number cards count at face value, face cards count as 10, and an Ace counts as 1 or 11—whichever helps your hand.

Blackjack

An Ace with a 10-value card on the opening deal. It pays 3:2; fractional chips round down.

Dealer

The dealer draws to 16 and stands on every 17, including a soft 17.

Double Down

On your first decision, double your bet and receive exactly one more card.

Push

If you and the dealer finish with the same total, your wager is returned.

Keyboard: H to hit, S to stand, D to double, and Enter or Space to deal or play again.
