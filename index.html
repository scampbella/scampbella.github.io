Currently Listening

Album of the Week

Preacher's Daughter

by Ethel Cain

Released May 12, 2022 · 75:42

It wasn't until the past few months that I listened to Preacher's Daughter, but boy am I glad I did. I very vaguely know the lore surrounding Ethel Cain, but having a story at all is pretty uncommon these days as far as I know. Having a story to loosely follow is cool and I really enjoy being able to listen to the songs and be like "oh I know what the house in Nebraska was!" That kinda stuff is just fun. It makes me feel a bit more connected to the music.

A lot of the songs on this album are epic. They have such sprawling soundscapes and you can really feel the vibe in your whole body. It's so specific and beautiful. Most of the songs aren't very heartwarming or anything remotely similar, but music doesn't have to be happy to make you feel good. It's so damn calming. When I listen to this album it feels as though I'm floating in a bathtub under candlelight, staring at the ceiling with no thoughts. Just feeling and seeing, nothing more.

I don't really have any personal anecdote for this album as I've only really started listening to it recently. I really do love it though, it's such a great vibe this time of year too. As the rain comes in and it gets darker earlier, the world feels moodier. When I close my eyes, all I see is her walking down a country road all alone in the dark as the rain comes down.

I wouldn't say this album is perfect though, there are a couple songs that overdo it a bit for my taste. Ptolemaea and August Underground are a bit TOO vibey for me. They stray a bit more towards the intense spooky vibe and throw me off a bit. That's not to say they're bad, just not for me really. Aside from that though the music is absolutely great. I appreciate how long the songs are. I feel like a lot of music these days just doesn't capitalize on itself enough if that makes sense. There are so many great songs that are written just too short. I constantly find myself thinking that if the song was just a bit longer it would feel more satisfying, though this thought never crosses my mind with Preacher's Daughter.

Favorites here are Western Nights, Hard Times, and Thoroughfare. Go listen!

About

Bio

I am a data science student at Oregon State University and am interested in exploring the field as much as I can. I love how data science can be applied to any field. It’s perfect for me because I like to work on varied projects and want to be able to keep learning and building skills throughout my career. I want to be able to apply my skills to any problem that is presented, and that is what I find so exciting about data science.

I have experience working with EEG signals to create visualization and exploration tools. These tools help people of all experience levels explore the most complex organ in the human body: the brain! This taught me how important communicating is, since information is no good if the people who need it don’t understand it. This has been apparent in the Computer Hardware Club that I run, where we teach complex topics to members with no experience.

Career Goals

My career goal is to become a data science consultant. I would like to start on a small team where I can work on the entire pipeline of a project first to get serious and in-depth experience before moving into consulting. I am most interested in the variety that consulting provides since it will allow me to express my skills in different ways and keep learning all throughout my career. Regardless of what project I work on, I’ll be able to learn about the subject hands-on and investigate before applying my data science skills and coming up with a solution.

Another reason I’m interested in consulting is because it is very heavy in communication and I have found that very rewarding in the past. In my club I have run workshops that required me to teach complex topics to people of different skill levels. I’ve had to figure out how to explain things most effectively for different people. I’ve had to find out how they learned best and adapt to their needs. Seeing members of my club go from being brand new at a subject to coming back the next year and showing off a project they made with that information is absolutely amazing. That same process is exactly how consulting operates and that’s why I want to do it.
